# YAKBD Editor VERSION (preview)

With yakbd CLI_VERSION.

## Downloads

- **Windows x64**: `yakbd-setup.exe` (editor and `yakbd.exe` in one folder; replaces a CLI-only install in place;
  `/quiet /norestart` for unattended installs) or the portable zip.
- **macOS** (Apple silicon and Intel): `yakbd-gui-VERSION-macos-universal.dmg`. The app is not signed or notarized.
  The first time, macOS refuses to open it: open it once, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, click
  **Open Anyway** next to the message about YAKBD (within about an hour), and confirm.
- **Linux x86-64**: the AppImage, or the tarball (`bin/yakbd-gui`). glibc 2.35 or later.
- **Source of the AppImage runtime** (LGPL-2.1 libfuse inside it): `type2-runtime-20251108-source.tar.gz` and
  `fuse-3.15.0.tar.xz`, attached to this release (see NOTICE). Qt's source: the download.qt.io URLs in NOTICE.

Check a download against `SHA256SUMS`.
